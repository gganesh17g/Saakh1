## Phased commencement

Rules 1, 2 and 17–21 are the immediate group; Rule 4 is the one-year group; Rules 3, 5–16, 22 and 23 the eighteen-month group (Rule 1). The Act commencement notification separately lists immediate provisions, section 6(9)/27(1)(d) after one year, and remaining enumerated provisions after eighteen months. As of Oct 27, 2026 the November 2025 one-year and eighteen-month periods have not elapsed. Confirm exact Official Gazette publication date and subsequent changes with counsel; do not assume the whole scheme is already operative or irrelevant to future launch. Other applicable laws are not displaced by a demo label.

## Controls and gaps

| Area | Implemented local behavior | Remaining production requirement |
|---|---|---|
| Notice / purpose (Rule 3 readiness) | Public privacy page; no real credentials requested; separate purpose/scope/recipient/expiry records | Identify actual fiduciary/processor, lawful basis, itemized notices, consent evidence, languages and rights channels against full Act/current rules |
| Withdrawal | Revocation timestamp/audit; no adapter transmits externally | Comparable-ease withdrawal, partner propagation, purpose enforcement and legally justified retention |
| Security (Rule 6 readiness) | Input schemas, business/function checks, file size/type limits, atomic snapshot writes | Auth/session, database tenant isolation, protected objects, encryption, monitoring, backups, contracts and access review |
| Breach (Rule 7 readiness) | No production breach workflow or notifications | Appoint accountable owner and legally reviewed detection/escalation/notification process; test deadlines/communications under applicable commencement |
| Retention (Rule 8 readiness) | User can remove owned demo keys; synthetic records only | Retention schedule, legal holds and processor deletion/backup policy. Rule 8 contains class/schedule and processing-retention provisions: blanket instant erasure is not a compliant production policy |
| Contact / rights (Rule 9 and Act review pending) | Reports export, privacy/settings/help disclosures | Real contact, identity checks, correction/access/grievance case handling and accountable response tracking |
| Consent manager | No registration or external consent-manager operation | Do not confuse a local form with registered Consent Manager status or AA consent artefact |

Public demo safeguards reduce unnecessary data collection; they do not make browser storage secure. Deleting records in one browser cannot delete downloaded files, other devices, hosting logs or downstream copies. Browser audit is editable. No child-data onboarding or identity verification exists; use synthetic examples only. Production requires a complete legal applicability review, including the full Act and current notifications.
