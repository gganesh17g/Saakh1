# Payroll methodology · payroll-paise-1

Money is stored in integer paise. Effective salary versions and rule IDs are snapshotted, together with employee name/department/designation and reviewed attendance, into each payroll line. Archiving an employee never removes a finalized run. A salary version at an existing effective date cannot be changed. Mid-month changes for existing employees are rejected rather than silently choosing an inaccurate blended salary; split-period payroll is not implemented.

Monthly recurring basic/HRA/allowances/fixed and performance incentives use calendar-day joining proration multiplied by `(working − unpaid) / working`. Present + paid leave + unpaid must equal 1–31 reviewed working days. Joining proration counts calendar days from joining date to month end, inclusive. Period bonus, fixed bonus and arrears are explicit non-prorated earnings; overtime is entered integer hours × entered hourly paise. Reimbursement is separate from gross. Leave entitlement is reference information, not an automatic leave accrual or minimum-wage check.

Gross = sum earnings. Employee deductions = configured EPF + ESI + manually entered professional tax/withholding/authorized deductions + evidenced advance recovery. Net = gross + reimbursement − deductions. Employer cost = gross + reimbursement + employer contributions. EPF basis is prorated basic capped at the entered cap (zero means uncapped); ESI basis is gross only when the employee toggle is enabled and the entered ceiling is not exceeded (zero means no ceiling). Administrator-supplied percentages convert to integer basis points. `roundedRatio` uses BigInt products and half-up rational rounding; no binary decimal division is used for payroll percentages/proration. This model is not a statutory wage/ESI continuation/EPS or tax eligibility engine.

Draft → Calculated or Needs Review → Approved → confirmed Finalized → Partially Paid/Paid. Changed attendance requires recalculation before approval. Approved attendance and finalized snapshots are locked. Duplicate active runs for a month and duplicate payment references are blocked. Future actual payroll periods are rejected. Correction reverses payments first, then the original finalized journal, retaining source snapshots and reversal references. A new run may follow a reversed run.

Finalization: Dr salaryExpense (gross + reimbursements), Dr employerExpense; Cr salaryPayable (net), deductionPayable (employee deductions excluding advance recovery), employerPayable, employeeAdvance (recovery). Payment: Dr salaryPayable, Cr bank/cash; no second salary expense. Advance: Dr employeeAdvance, Cr bank; recovery cannot exceed recorded outstanding advances. Deduction/employer remittances separately debit their own liabilities and credit bank, without claiming any submission to an authority.

The validator reconciles net = gross + reimbursement − deductions and employer cost, balanced journals, payment evidence, nonnegative outstanding and aggregate finalized salary source liabilities against the general ledger. Payslips require finalized non-reversed snapshots, show actual locally recorded payment status, and agree with those snapshots. Zero default statutory rates explicitly do not assert legal exemption.
