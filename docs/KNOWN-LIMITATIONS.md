# Known limitations — 27 October 2026

This is a functional, local-first synthetic-data demonstration, not a production financial service.

- No backend, secure authentication/session, server tenant isolation, durable multi-user authorization, account recovery, rate limits, encrypted managed storage, backups or production secret management. Demo role checks can be bypassed with browser tools. Membership scopes/expiry describe invitations; they do not create enforced authenticated sessions.
- English UI; Hindi/Marathi are stored preferences only. Some labels are technical and dates remain ISO. Full translation dictionaries, editable business settings and locale-format dates are unfinished.
- Documents support multi-line drafts, posting, unpaid corrections, partial payments, proportional credits/returns, cancellation and bank refunds. Item-selective returns, period locks, statutory invoice formats, invoice sending, payment-gateway processing and advanced debit-note/overpayment workflows are absent.
- Standard-cost inventory with expense price variance is illustrative. It is not weighted-average valuation, GST accounting advice or a validated tax inventory method. Negative cash can represent a recorded overdraft; no bank limit is checked.
- Uploads are retained locally with size/MIME limits and SHA-256 digest. No OCR, malware inspection, server protected download or authenticity verification. Use synthetic files only. Orphan attachments may remain after abandoning an editor.
- Statement imports accept bounded CSV and first-sheet XLSX columns A–C. Dates must be ISO text; serial dates, formulas, XLS/XLSM, arbitrary column mapping and PDF extraction are unsupported. Multi-journal split matching exists; splitting a single book journal across multiple statement rows does not.
- Data health is a set of counts and ratios, not a credit score or verification. Completeness, missing periods and source freshness require user judgement. Invoice search/filtering, historical cash overlay and all KPI drilldowns are not exhaustive.
- Forecasts use a constant future level from the best one-step baseline. No calibrated uncertainty, reliable seasonal model, feature-rich regression, weekly/monthly aggregation or confirmed supplier-term negotiation. Data omissions and partial periods can bias backtests. Saved snapshot actual comparison waits until its end date.
- Funding is indicative mathematics with sample method comparisons. No live offers, lender identity/KFS/APR, regulated product validation, referral commissions, application tracking or loan approval. No automated consequential decision.
- Marketplace has RFQs, quotes, delivered-cost ordering, shortlist, local outcomes and report/block/moderation. Structured catalogues/MOQ/delivery-range, enquiry messaging, fulfilment, repeat-trade analytics and comprehensive liquidity metrics remain partial. No orders are sent.
- Tax tasks are manually dated internal readiness tasks. No GST return preparation engine, tax-payment posting workflow, ITC check, statutory due-date service, government submission or Filed status.
- Assistant is a deterministic query dispatcher with evidence references, not an LLM, voice assistant or tax/legal adviser. Unsupported requests return a bounded explanation; natural language never writes the ledger.
- Client audit is inspectable but mutable. Single-snapshot atomic writes do not prevent concurrent multi-tab lost updates. Ledger and credit keys are not transactionally linked. Deletion removes only this browser’s owned saved keys; backups/downloads/other devices are outside its scope.
- Initial npm audit reported four findings. Zod was upgraded to 4.6.7 and Vitest to 5.0.5 after compatibility checks; the final clean-install audit reports zero vulnerabilities. This does not certify application security. Recharts 2 and ESLint 9 deprecation notices remain; see TEST-REPORT.md.
- Production build keeps a large lazy WebGL chunk. Responsive and software-WebGL checks are not mobile hardware, accessibility certification, FPS or production-load benchmarks.
- Live GitHub Pages publication of this upgrade has not been performed. A valid local production build does not establish that the public URL serves it.

Regulatory readiness is partial, not compliance. Official DPDP rules, commencement notifications and corrigendum were read, but the Act’s full consolidated text fetch timed out; current supersession and role-specific legal interpretation require qualified review. The RBI AA page itself advertises a September 2024 update; later amendments have not been exhaustively excluded. All external adapters remain unavailable.
